About league bowling

You don’t have to be great at bowling to join a league. We have leagues for bowlers of all ages and skill levels. If you are new, please don’t feel intimidated by other league bowlers; everyone used to be a recreational bowler just like you.

At Highland Park Lanes, our experienced staff make league bowling a great way to improve your game, meet new people, and most importantly, have fun!
